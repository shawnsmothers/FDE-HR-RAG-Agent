1. Welcome to Northstar
Who we are. Northstar Distribution & Fulfillment is a fictional business-to-business warehousing and distribution company. We receive
finished consumer and industrial products from manufacturers, store inventory, pick and pack customer orders, and ship products to
regional and national retailers, commercial customers, and local businesses.
Mission. To move every order safely, accurately, and on time while creating a workplace where employees are treated with respect,
understand what is expected of them, and have the tools to do quality work.
Core values. Safety first; show up ready; protect quality; own the result; respect people; communicate early; improve the process.

Attendance & Point System
Reliable attendance is an essential job expectation. Employees must be at their assigned work area, ready to work, at the scheduled
start time. Points are assessed per occurrence unless the absence is approved, protected by law, covered by an approved leave, or
otherwise excused in writing.
Attendance Event Points Example / Rule
Late arrival under 2 hours 0.5 Clocking in 1-119 minutes after scheduled start
Late arrival 2 hours or more 1.0 Clocking in 120+ minutes late without approved schedule change
Early departure after second scheduled break 0.5 Employee works most of shift but leaves early after the second break
Early departure before second scheduled break 1.0 Employee leaves a substantial portion of the shift unworked
Full scheduled day missed - proper call-off 2.0 Employee reports the absence under the call-off procedure
No-call / no-show 4.0 Highest single attendance occurrence; employee neither reports nor appears for the shift
Two consecutive no-call / no-show shifts 8.0 4 points per shift; may also trigger job-abandonment review
Point expiration: Each point occurrence remains active for 12 months from the date of the event and then falls off automatically.
Example: a 1-point event dated March 10, 2026 expires March 10, 2027.
Corrective-action levels: 4 active points - documented coaching; 6 points - written warning; 9 points - final attendance warning;
12 active points - termination of employment, subject to HR review. Management may address separate misconduct, falsification,
job abandonment, or safety violations outside the point system.
Call-off procedure. Employees should notify the designated attendance line or supervisor as soon as practicable before the shift. A
coworker is not an approved substitute for notifying management. Emergencies that prevent timely notice will be reviewed individually.
Protected and approved time. Approved PTO, approved leaves, jury duty, bereavement when provided, military leave, reasonable
accommodations, and other legally protected absences are not assessed attendance points when the employee follows applicable
notice/documentation requirements.

4. Paid Time Off (PTO)
PTO may be used for vacation, personal time, appointments, and illness unless another leave program applies. PTO is paid at the
employee's applicable base rate and is not treated as hours worked for overtime calculations unless required by law or a written
company plan.
Service Level PTO Benefit Administration
Hire date through first anniversary 3 hours per completed biweekly pay period Up to 78 hours over 26 full pay periods
1 year through under 2 years 85 hours per year Annual bank credited at service anniversary
2 years through under 3 years 95 hours per year Annual bank credited at service anniversary
3+ years 100 hours per year Annual bank credited at service anniversary
Important: In this sample policy, the annual 85/95/100-hour bank replaces the first-year 3-hours-per-pay-period method after the
applicable service anniversary; the two benefits do not stack.
Requests and approval. Planned PTO should normally be requested at least 7 calendar days in advance. Approval depends on
staffing, workload, blackout periods, and order of requests. Unplanned PTO does not by itself erase attendance points unless the
absence is approved/excused under policy.
Carryover. Up to 40 unused PTO hours may carry into the next benefit year, subject to applicable law. Employees should use PTO
regularly rather than accumulate excessive balances. Payout of unused PTO at separation is governed by applicable law and the
written plan in effect at the time of separation.
5. Paid Holidays
Eligible full-time employees receive the following company-paid holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor
Day, Thanksgiving Day, the Friday after Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day. The published holiday calendar controls when an observed
date differs from the calendar holiday.
Operations employees: Distribution operations may remain open on some holidays. Employees scheduled to work must follow the
posted holiday schedule and will receive the holiday treatment announced for that year. Office/executive employees: Offices are
normally closed on designated holidays unless business coverage is required. Holiday eligibility may require working the scheduled shift
immediately before and after the holiday unless the absence is approved or protected.
6. Safety, Accidents & Incident Reporting
Employees must immediately address emergencies and report work-related injuries, near misses, property damage, product damage,
vehicle incidents, spills, unsafe conditions, and security events as soon as practicable.
Step Employee / Manager Action
1. Protect people Stop work when needed, obtain first aid/emergency assistance, and secure the area without creating additional risk.
2. Notify Tell the supervisor, manager, Safety, or HR as soon as practicable.
3. Document Complete an Incident Report with date/time, location, people involved, witnesses, equipment/product involved, and factual descripti4. Preserve facts Do not alter the scene or discard relevant items unless necessary for safety or continued emergency response.
5. Review Management/Safety investigates causes, determines corrective actions, and completes any required recordkeeping/reporting.
Employees have the right to report work-related injuries and illnesses without retaliation. Safety reporting must not be
discouraged by attendance or incentive rules. Managers are responsible for escalating serious incidents and contacting
Safety/HR for any legally required external reporting.
Production PPE: Required PPE may include safety footwear, high-visibility garments, gloves, hearing protection, eye protection, and
task-specific equipment. Only trained and authorized employees may operate powered industrial equipment. Horseplay, bypassing
guards, or defeating safety devices is prohibited.

7. Timekeeping, Breaks & Overtime
Hourly employees must record their own time accurately and may not clock in/out for another employee. Off-the-clock work is
prohibited. Production schedules may include designated meal and rest periods. Employees must return from breaks on time. Overtime
must be authorized, but all hours actually worked must still be reported and paid in accordance with applicable law.
8. Standards of Conduct
Employees are expected to work safely; follow lawful instructions; treat coworkers, customers, and vendors professionally; protect
company/customer property; maintain accurate records; and avoid conflicts of interest. Theft, violence, threats, harassment, deliberate
falsification, working under the influence, serious safety violations, or intentional misuse of confidential information may result in
discipline up to termination.
9. Respectful Workplace & Equal Opportunity
Northstar's sample policy prohibits discrimination, harassment, and retaliation based on legally protected status. Concerns may be
reported to a supervisor, another manager, HR, or the designated ethics channel. Employees do not have to report a concern to a
person involved in the concern. Retaliation for a good-faith report or participation in an investigation is prohibited.
10. Technology, Confidentiality & Data
Company systems are primarily for authorized business use. Employees must protect passwords, customer information, pricing,
employee records, financial information, vendor terms, operational data, and other confidential information. Unapproved software,
external storage, or disclosure of company data is prohibited. AI tools may only be used for company information when approved by IT
and consistent with data-security requirements.
11. Weather, Emergencies & Facility Closures
Employees should not assume a facility is closed because of severe weather. Northstar will communicate closures, delayed starts, or
emergency instructions through designated channels. Employees should use reasonable judgment about personal safety and
communicate promptly when travel conditions affect attendance.
12. Performance, Coaching & Corrective Action
Performance is evaluated based on role expectations such as safety, quality, productivity, attendance, teamwork, customer service,
leadership, accuracy, and policy compliance. Corrective action may include coaching, written warnings, final warnings, suspension
where permitted, or termination. The company may skip steps depending on severity, history, or circumstances, subject to applicable
law and written agreements.
